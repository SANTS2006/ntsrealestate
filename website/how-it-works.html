How it works

Live on your portfolio, step by step

We handle set-up with you, so your team spends its time managing property — not learning software.

Onboarding

From first call to first invoice

A guided rollout that matches how your business already works.

Start on WhatsApp
Step 1

Discovery call

We learn about your portfolio, team, roles and reporting needs, and walk you through the platform.

Step 2

Workspace set-up

Your organisation is created and branded. Roles and permissions are configured for your team structure.

Step 3

Data onboarding

Properties, buildings, units, owners, tenants and active leases are added so day-one data is accurate.

Step 4

Team training

Short, role-based sessions for managers, finance staff and agents, plus guidance for inviting tenants.

Step 5

Go live & support

You start invoicing, collecting and resolving requests. We stay on hand through WhatsApp, phone and email.

Architecture

Simple, modern and maintained

Four layers, each with a clear job — and no servers of your own to run.

Web app

A fast React single-page application that works on desktop and mobile and can be installed to the home screen.

API

A Node.js / Express service with validated inputs, rate limiting, structured logging and security headers.

Database

Managed PostgreSQL on Neon, accessed through Prisma, with Postgres-backed sessions.

File storage

Documents live in Cloudinary with private, authenticated delivery for sensitive files.

Day to day

A typical month on the platform

Week 1

Invoice

Rent invoices are issued against active leases. Tenants see what's due in their portal.

Weeks 2–3

Collect & resolve

Payments are recorded as they arrive; maintenance requests become work orders and are closed out.

Week 4

Report

Occupancy, collection and maintenance reports go to owners, backed by the audit log.
