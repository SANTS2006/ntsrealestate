Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp at +232 31 034 604.

Who is the NTS Real Estate System for?

Property management companies, landlords, agencies, developers and agents — and their owners, tenants and vendors. It supports multiple organisations on one platform, each with its own isolated data.

What can I manage with it?

Properties, buildings, units, owners, tenants, leases, invoices, payments, expenses, maintenance requests, work orders, vendors, inspections, documents, land and deals, agents and referrals, chat, notifications and reports.

Do my tenants get access?

Yes. Tenants have a portal where they can see their lease and rentals, view payments, report maintenance issues and message the manager.

How are payments handled?

You record payments against invoices using methods such as cash, bank transfer, card, mobile money or cheque. The system prevents duplicate and over-payments and supports refunds with proper reversal.

Is my data secure?

Data is isolated per organisation, access is role-based, passwords use Argon2id, MFA is available, documents are privately stored and key actions are logged.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It's a responsive web application that works on phones, tablets and computers, and can be installed to your home screen.

Can it match my company's branding?

Each organisation can apply its own logo and colours throughout the application.

Can you migrate my existing data?

We help onboard your properties, units, owners, tenants and leases during set-up. Tell us what you have today and we'll advise on the best way to bring it in.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your portfolio size and the modules you need. Request a quote and we'll send a clear proposal.
