Who we are

Information we collect on this website

When you use the Contact or Request-a-demo form, we receive and store the details you enter (such as your name, company, email address, phone number, portfolio size and message), along with the page you sent it from and your IP address, which we use to prevent abuse. These details are stored in our database, emailed to our platform administrators and kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and follow up.

How we use information

Customer organisations are responsible for the personal data they enter about their owners, tenants and staff. We process that data on their behalf to provide the service, protected by the measures described on our Security page.
