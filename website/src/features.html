Features

Every tool a property business needs

Twenty-plus modules that share one database, one permission model and one audit trail — so nothing falls between the cracks.

{{i:building}}
Portfolio

Properties & buildings

Register properties with their structure, status and buildings. Assign managers so people only see what they're responsible for.

{{i:layers}}
Portfolio

Units

Track every unit's status — available, reserved, occupied or under maintenance — and link it to its building, lease and tenant.

{{i:key}}
Portfolio

Owners

Keep owner records and the properties they hold, with a transparent trail of income, expenses and activity.

{{i:file}}
Portfolio

Secure documents

Attach title deeds, IDs, leases, invoices and photos to the record they belong to. Files are stored privately and served only to authorised users.

{{i:users}}
Leasing

Tenant management

Full tenant profiles, rental history and status — from applicant to move-out.

{{i:file}}
Leasing

Lease lifecycle

Create, activate, renew and end leases with clear statuses so you always know what is current and what is expiring.

{{i:mobile}}
Leasing

Tenant portal

Tenants view their lease, see payments, track their rentals, report issues and message the manager — on any device.

{{i:receipt}}
Finance

Invoicing

Issue invoices against leases and follow each through draft, sent, partially paid, paid or overdue.

{{i:wallet}}
Finance

Payments & refunds

Record cash, bank transfer, card, mobile money or cheque. Idempotent processing blocks duplicates and over-payment; refunds reverse cleanly.

{{i:coins}}
Finance

Expenses & approvals

Categorise costs and route them through an approval workflow before they hit the books.

{{i:chart}}
Finance

Financial summary

A consolidated view of income, expenses and collections to support owner reporting and decisions.

{{i:wrench}}
Operations

Maintenance requests

Tenants and staff log issues with a priority. Managers triage, assign and close them with a full history.

{{i:clipboard}}
Operations

Work orders

Turn requests into work orders, assign a vendor and record completion details and costs.

{{i:users}}
Operations

Vendor directory

Keep contractors and suppliers in one place and link them to the jobs they perform.

{{i:eye}}
Operations

Inspections

Move-in, move-out, routine, property and unit inspections — each with a status and a record you can refer back to.

{{i:land}}
Land & deals

Land register

List land parcels with their status and documents, ready to rent, lease or sell.

{{i:tag}}
Land & deals

Deals & instalments

Manage rent, lease and purchase deals, with instalment schedules for buyers paying over time.

{{i:network}}
Land & deals

Agents, referrals & network

{{i:chat}}

{{i:mail}}

{{i:chart}}
